A practical framework for explaining reward value at the moment a customer makes a purchase.

Set the expectation before checkout.

Customers should not first discover how rewards work at the final step. Introduce the available payment choices while people are exploring the experience so they understand what is possible.

Put it into practice

Explain whether points cover the whole purchase or can contribute towards it, according to the program rules.

Show one coherent price story.

A hybrid payment journey can introduce several numbers: the purchase price, points contribution, remaining payment, and any fees. Present them as one clear calculation rather than disconnected pieces of information.

Put it into practice

Use a consistent order and keep the amount still payable visually distinct.

Explain change and cancellation outcomes.

A customer’s decision includes what happens if plans change. A useful journey makes the treatment of points and money understandable before commitment, based on the terms of the booking and program.

Put it into practice

Agree refund messaging with the teams responsible for booking operations and program rules.

Review the whole experience.

Price clarity is only one part of the journey. Availability, validation, confirmation, and support all contribute to whether the customer trusts the transaction.

Put it into practice

Walk through a successful purchase, an unavailable option, an interrupted payment, and a change request before launch.
