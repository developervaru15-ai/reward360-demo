About R360

Behind the experiences.
Built around people.

We bring technology, commerce, and customer understanding together to make loyalty part of everyday life.

Technology. Operations. Customer experience.

A more rewarding world starts with a more useful relationship.

R360 works at the intersection of loyalty, lifestyle commerce, and financial technology. We help businesses connect their customer relationships with experiences people can discover, choose, and use.

That means looking beyond the reward itself. It means considering the journey, the intelligence behind it, and the operations that turn a promise into an experience.

A story of expanding possibilities.

From merchandise and voucher fulfilment to a connected loyalty and commerce ecosystem.

2010–2015

The foundations of loyalty.

Merchandise and voucher fulfilment established the operational base for a broader rewards business.

2016–2022

Digital capabilities. Wider reach.

R360 developed its technology platform, added travel operations, and expanded into more markets.

2023 onwards

A more connected proposition.

Marketplace, premium brand experiences, and lifestyle concierge broadened the ways customers can use rewards.
