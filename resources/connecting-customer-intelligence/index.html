A focused approach to using customer understanding in ways that serve both the audience and the business.

Begin with a decision worth improving.

A data initiative becomes easier to evaluate when it starts with a real decision. Which customer needs a reminder? Which reward should appear next? Which moment needs less communication?

Put it into practice

Choose a single use case before expanding the scope of data collection or activation.

Understand the signal’s limits.

A transaction can suggest a need without proving it. A browsing action can indicate interest without implying intent. Treat signals as context, and consider what the customer has actually chosen to share.

Put it into practice

Identify uncertainty and agree how it should affect the experience.

Make the action relevant.

The next interaction should offer a clear benefit to the customer. More precise targeting is not enough if the message, offer, or recommendation does not help someone do something they value.

Put it into practice

Define the customer benefit alongside the business objective for every journey.

Build a review habit.

Useful personalisation needs regular assessment. Review not only response but also frequency, repetition, and whether the interaction still fits the context in which it appears.

Put it into practice

Set a review cadence and decide in advance when a journey should be adjusted or paused.
