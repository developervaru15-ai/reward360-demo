Planning report

The connected experience planning brief.

A working framework for shaping a loyalty, commerce, or travel proposition.

Book a demo →Talk to our team →
In this reportStart with the customer relationship.Define the value exchange.Map the connected journey.Agree the operating model.Choose a learning framework.

Planning framework. Not a research report or product specification.

01

Start with the customer relationship.

Describe the audience, the current experience, and the moment that needs to become more useful. A proposition should begin with a clear customer need rather than a list of available features.

02

Define the value exchange.

Explain what participation asks from the customer and what it makes possible in return. Consider practical value, aspirational value, and the clarity of the earning and redemption story.

03

Map the connected journey.

Review entry, discovery, eligibility, transaction, confirmation, and assistance. Identify where information must remain consistent and where responsibility moves between teams.

04

Agree the operating model.

Define who owns program rules, supply, customer data, records, service, and exceptions. Bring business, technical, and operational perspectives into the same discussion.

05

Choose a learning framework.

Agree a small set of customer and business measures before implementation. Establish the baseline, the review cadence, and the decisions the team will make when evidence changes.
